--- 1. root cause of the round-1 hang -------------------------

factor flips s -> r-s when r is odd and s is even, so that fastsqr can be
used.  For -s0 588 at r=136279841 the *working* trinomial is therefore
x^r + x^136279253 + 1, i.e. s > r/2 with r-s = 588.

The round-1 ec_reduce_ref reduced by repeated block folding, whose degree
drops by (r-s) per pass.  Pass count = (r-1)/(r-s) + 1:
   r=136279841, s=       345  ->         2 passes
   r=136279841, s=       588  ->         2 passes
   r=136279841, s= 136279253  ->    231769 passes

   gecsim used r=1279, s=345 -> 2 passes, which is why it was clean.
